3 Places to Visit in Pune

Shaniwar Wada

A historic fort built in 1732 by the Peshwas of the Maratha Empire. It is famous for its huge gates and the evening light and sound show.

Aga Khan Palace

A beautiful palace where Mahatma Gandhi was kept during the Quit India Movement. Today it is a museum and memorial with lovely gardens.

Sinhagad Fort

A hill fort about 35 km from Pune, known for the brave battle of Tanaji Malusare. It is a popular spot for trekking and great views.
